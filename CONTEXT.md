# Cards Against Bhayanak

## Language

**Room**:
A gathering identified by one join code, whose memberships remain available across replays.
_Avoid_: Match, game session

**Membership**:
One participant identity within a room. A participant who permanently leaves and joins again receives a new membership, even when reusing the same handle.

**Match**:
One playthrough in a room, with its own numbered rounds, configuration, final scores and outcome. A rematch starts another match in the same room.
_Avoid_: Room
